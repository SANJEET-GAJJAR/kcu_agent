# Cascade Confirmation — 3-of-3 Rule ★★★

#heatseeker #management #node

## The 3-Part Rule

All three required for a STRETCH hold (runner past PT1):

| Condition | What to look for |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **1. +GEX node grows THROUGH the break** | The node price broke through is growing (not shrinking) = cascade fuel, not a floor |
| **2. Stacked negative nodes beneath** | Negative nodes below the break and growing = acceleration fuel |
| **3. King shrinking** | The dominant ceiling node is weakening = less pull-back pressure |

---

## Sizing by Confirmation Count

| Score | Size rule |
|-------|-----------|
| **3-of-3** | Hold runner, stretch to next fuel node |
| **2-of-3** | PT1 scalp only — do NOT hold for stretch |
| **1-of-3** | Scalp only, very tight |
| **0-of-3** | Don't enter expecting a cascade |

> [!warning]
> A valid LTP entry + valid PC does NOT automatically earn a stretch hold.
> Cascade confirmation is a SEPARATE check that gates the runner decision.

---

## CONFIRMED Pattern: Cascade-Fuel-Then-Floor-Strengthening (11 examples)

**What it looks like:**
1. +GEX node grows THROUGH a break (from above) = cascade fuel ✓
2. Same node may later GROW while price bases at it = floor strengthening ✓

**The full cycle in one trade:**
- Phase 1: Node grows through break → target is BELOW the node
- Phase 2: Same node grows while price bases → new floor, cascade over

**Key examples:**
- Trade 001 (TSLA 2026-06-18): +390 grew 2.6M→5.1M through break = cascade fuel
- Trade 024 (TSLA 2026-03-25): 387.5 exploded -$4.1M→-$15.2M, then decayed 94% → fuel then exit
- Trade 028/029 (TSLA 2026-03-27): SAME nodes, same session — floor at 9:38 → fuel at 9:46

## When King is the Breaking Node

> [!warning]
> When King node = the level being broken, do NOT treat a flat King reading as "it will hold."
> A flat King snapshot can surge the moment price breaks through it = King is the fuel.
> Must re-check King's trend at EVERY new leg, not just once at entry.

---

## Failure Condition (cascade dies early)

Watch for within first 1-4 min after entry:
1. At-spot node (immediately below price for puts) flips from negative toward POSITIVE
2. Adjacent fuel nodes drain instead of growing
3. King rebuilds (ceiling strengthening while cascade supposed to run)

When these appear: **tighten immediately, don't wait for price to tag the stop.**

---

## CANDIDATE: Bullish Reversal — Two-Part Signal (2 examples → need 1 more)

For calling a cascade BOTTOM, need BOTH simultaneously:
1. Nearest -GEX node peaks in magnitude, then **immediately starts draining**
2. King's rate of decline **decelerates sharply** (doesn't need to fully bottom)

Lead time: signal fires 1-4 min BEFORE actual price extreme. Use to tighten shorts/prepare for bounce-scalp entry, not to call the exact tick.
